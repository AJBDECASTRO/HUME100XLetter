HUME 100 CORE LESSON
How Everything Connects

The Circle of Balance

In Los Baños, every part of our town connects to every other part. What we build affects our forests, and the health of our forests directly determines our quality of life.

01. Our People

Mutual aid (Bayanihan), weekly assemblies, and intergenerational voting ensure every voice counts.

02. Our Buildings

Bio-concrete spires, solar windows, and shaded bridges that cool the town and eliminate traffic smog.

03. Our Nature

Continuous canopy corridors connecting Mount Makiling's cloud forests all the way to Laguna de Bay's clean shoreline.

04. Nature's Limits

Living strictly within the daily water and energy capacity that nature regenerates, preventing ecological collapse.

Four Core Pillars

The Foundations of Human Ecology

The four guiding principles taught at UPLB that helped Los Baños thrive through mid-century climate challenges.

Humans and Nature as One Living System

A city is not separate from its environment. It is an organ of the landscape. What harms Mount Makiling's watershed directly impacts our drinking water, and what revitalizes the lake restores our food supply.

Living Within Ecological Carrying Capacity

Rather than consuming endlessly, our community matches its daily resource use to what the sun, rainfall, and geothermal systems naturally regenerate each day.

Community Equity and Bayanihan

A truly sustainable city cannot be an exclusive enclave for the rich. Clean air, fresh water, and storm-resilient housing are community rights shared equally through cooperative ownership and mutual aid.

Intergenerational Responsibility

Decisions are evaluated on a 50-to-100-year horizon. Youth councils hold permanent seats in municipal governance, ensuring long-term stewardship over quick short-term convenience.

Comparison

Two Different Futures for Cities

How our Human Ecology model in Los Baños compares to standard corporate "smart cities".

FEATURE CORPORATE SMART CITY (OLD WAY) LOS BAÑOS 2055 (HUMAN ECOLOGY)
Water & Power Sold for profit; the rich waste resources while the poor suffer cuts. Shared fairly based on nature's daily limits; neighbors help each other.
Buildings Concrete and glass that trap heat and need constant air conditioning. Self-healing green towers that absorb carbon and let mountain winds blow through.
Nature Pushed aside and treated as an afterthought or decoration. The true foundation of our town; forest corridors run right through our buildings.
Housing Expensive luxury apartments that push local families away. Community-owned vertical homes with guaranteed shelter for all residents.
Decisions Made by distant companies looking for quick profits. Decided by residents and young people in weekly community meetings.
