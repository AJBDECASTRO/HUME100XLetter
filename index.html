LOS BAÑOS, LAGUNA (2055) MOUNT MAKILING BIOPHILIC CORRIDORS UPLB ARCHIVAL RECORD
DISPATCH 01 // MUNICIPAL COUNCIL ARCHIVE SEPTEMBER 11, 2055

Dearest Earth Citizen,

By 2055, Los Baños has transformed into a vertical, biophilic eco-city where compact urban hubs are closely integrated with restored Makiling forest corridors, yet resource sharing remains an active daily commitment for its residents.

From the high-rise terraces of Sector 4, the separation between human shelter and wild nature has disappeared. Our buildings are constructed with self-healing bio-concrete that repairs its own fractures naturally, while vertical gardens feed and cool every neighborhood.

Laguna de Bay gleams below—restored from decades of pollution by community-managed wetland reed filters. We invite you to see what human ecology can achieve when technology is guided by community values and environmental care.

— Sector 4 Resident Council, Los Baños

Three Core Pillars of the 2055 Transformation

How green engineering, community living, and natural lake restoration work together.

ENGINEERED INFRASTRUCTURE

Self-Healing Bio-Concrete

High-rise towers poured with concrete containing natural, harmless bacteria. When tiny cracks appear, moisture activates the bacteria to seal fractures with natural limestone, keeping buildings durable for generations.

Pillar 2 Picture Placeholder
File: images/eco_community.jpg
Community living or balcony breezeways picture
COMMUNITY LIVING

Canopy Breezeways

Shaded open-air communal terraces connecting high-rise residential towers. Residents meet weekly to share harvests from balcony gardens and democratically allocate daily water and power quotas.

File: images/laguna_de_bay.jpg
Natural restoration or Laguna Lake wetlands picture
NATURAL RESTORATION

Restored Laguna de Bay

A de-polluted lake ringed with a 300-meter buffer of native wetland reeds. Runoff water is naturally filtered before reaching the lake, providing safe waters for sustainable community fish farms.
