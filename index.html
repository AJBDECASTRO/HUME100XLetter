Dear People of 2025,

Hi. You can call me AJ. I am from the future, specifically 30 years from your current time. This letter’s purpose is to tell you what happened in Los Baños in the 3 decades from your time to now.

Area Development: By the year 2055, this place became unrecognizable, as Los Baños has transformed into a highly eco-friendly, futuristic, and biophilic town, where it has integrates many of the technological advancements the world had throughout the years, while also remaining true to its roots and embracing nature, as residential communites, bodies of water, and the glorious Mount Makiling are now living together as one interconnected system.

Quality of life: Life in this town is more than great. Other than it having one of the highest life expectancies in the country (82 years old), living life at the city is wonderful. Through integrating nature with the technological advancements, we now see a current with the convenience of technology while also enjoying breathing clean mountain air, walking along shaded tree corridors, and eating fresh vegetables from community gardeners.

State of natural and engineered environment : The state of both natural and engineered environment is also one of the most highly praised in the country. Steering away from traditional urbanization, structures were built using sturdy local bamboo and low-carbon materials cooled naturally by mountain breezes. Along with that, 85% of the municipality’s electricity are from solar grids, which fully represents the current development of Los Baños. Embracing technology while combining it with nature.

Human Ecology as a Framework: How has Human Ecology helped in all of this? The concept of interconnection between technology and nature is fundamentally follows a Human Ecological approach. Technology, built from social systems, alone cannot save the town from climate disasters. Nature alone will not cause development without proper utilization. Nature, a fundamental concept from the ecosystem, and Technology, which was born from the efforts of the social systems, must work together and exchange energy, material, and information to both save the world from climate destruction and also exhibit efficient development. Viewing this concepts as one big interconnected system rather than separate independent systems must be done in order make the world both safe and efficient and the human ecological framework provides beneficial view.

Bamboo Homes and Electricity

Rather than embracing full urbanization and making concrete structures, we embrace bamboo structures and utilize local resources for them. Other than that, electricity comes from clean grids, as 85% come from geothermal and solar energy.

Living As A System
