Dear 2025 Self & Fellow Citizens,

Area Description: By 2055, Los Baños has transformed into a compact, biophilic eco-town where residential communities and Mount Makiling’s rainforest corridors live together as one interconnected ecosystem.

Quality of Life: People live healthier, longer lives (averaging 82 years) breathing clean mountain air, walking along shaded tree corridors, eating fresh vegetables from community gardens, and sharing municipal water cooperatively through purok Bayanihan assemblies.

Natural & Engineered Environment: Rather than hot concrete sprawl, homes are built from sturdy local bamboo and low-carbon materials cooled naturally by mountain breezes. Shoreline wetlands filter domestic runoff into Laguna de Bay, and 85% of our electricity comes from clean geothermal and rooftop solar microgrids.

The Merit of Human Ecology: High-tech gadgets alone could never stop climate disasters. Los Baños survived because Human Ecology taught us to treat human communities, engineered buildings, and natural forests as one living system—balancing our daily consumption with what the watershed can naturally regenerate.
