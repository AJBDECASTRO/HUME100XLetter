Requirement 01

Quality of Life: Good Health and Shared Limits

Life in 2055 has two main sides: our bodies are healthier than ever, but we also live with strict rules on how much we can consume.

Great Public Health & Long Lives

HEALTH

Because there are no gas cars, trucks, or factory smoke, the air in Los Baños smells like mountain rain. Kids no longer suffer from asthma, and grandparents regularly live past 90 years old.

People walk everywhere along beautiful, shaded bridges connecting high-rise towers. Tower balconies are covered in sweet potato vines and tomatoes, giving families fresh food every day.

  • Clean Mountain Air: Cool breezes from Mount Makiling keep the town naturally fresh.
  • Cooling Domes: When severe heatwaves hit, public misting zones keep everyone safe from heat exhaustion.
  • Fresh Tower Produce: Every apartment building has community gardens growing fresh vegetables.

Fair Sharing & Community Stewardship

SHARED RULES

To make sure the town never runs dry, every neighborhood shares a daily water and clean power budget based on seasonal rainfall and solar generation.

Rather than letting wealthy individuals hoard resources while others suffer cuts, our community model ensures that clean drinking water, cooling, and electricity are guaranteed to every resident.

  • Daily Home Budget: Simple home displays help families see their daily use and avoid waste.
  • Community Sharing Pool: Unused daily credits are pooled to support families with newborn babies and elderly relatives.
  • Weekly Assemblies: Neighbors meet regularly to discuss rules, solve issues, and plan seasonal planting.
Requirement 02

Environment: Green Engineering & Restored Nature

Our technology does not destroy nature—it copies how nature heals itself.

Smart Green Engineering

SMART BUILDINGS

Self-Healing Concrete: Our high-rise towers are built with concrete containing harmless natural bacteria. When rain gets into a small crack, the bacteria wake up, create limestone, and seal the crack by themselves.

Underground Geothermal Power: We tap the natural heat deep inside Mount Makiling. This gives our town continuous, clean electricity that never goes out—even during heavy storms.

  • Carbon-Absorbing Walls: Building walls absorb greenhouse gases from the air.
  • Storm-Proof Power: Underground power lines are safe from typhoon winds.

Restoring the Mountain & Lake

HEALTHY NATURE

A Clean Laguna de Bay: The lake is no longer polluted. A 300-meter border of green wetland reeds and freshwater clams cleans all water naturally before it enters the lake.

Mount Makiling Forest: Over 12 million native trees were replanted. Skybridges let birds, monkeys, and deer travel freely between the mountain and the lake.

  • Natural Water Filters: Reeds and plants clean town runoff naturally without harsh chemicals.
  • Canopy Skybridges: High bridges allow animals to cross safely over roads and walkways.

Requirement 03

Community: Bayanihan and Fair Voice

How traditional Filipino values of helping neighbors (Bayanihan) keep our town fair and strong.

Bayanihan Sharing Pools

HELPING NEIGHBORS

Every apartment building has a community pool where families donate extra water or power credits they didn't use. If an elderly neighbor or a newborn baby needs extra water, it comes directly from this shared pool.

Residents take turns cleaning vertical gardens and maintaining solar panels together on weekends, building strong friendships across generations.

Youth Having a Real Voice

FUTURE GENERATIONS

Young residents aged 16 to 25 have a permanent seat on the town council. They hold veto power on any construction project that takes more than 10 years to build.

This guarantees that older leaders cannot make decisions that damage the environment for the children who will inherit the town.
