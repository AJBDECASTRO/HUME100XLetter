Core Ecosystem Criteria for 2055

HEALTH

Clean Air & Healthy Neighborhoods

Remove a large majority of smoke pollution and improving covers by planting trees. Has reduced diseases in humans.

Clean air: Electric Jeepneys and Solar Energy makes the air cleaner and more breathable.

Cool Shade Everywhere: Trees act as a cover to prevent the temperature from increasing drastically.

Walkable City: Shaded walking paths and bike lanes allow residents to reach places without much need for vehicles.

HOUSING

Naturally Cool Homes & Clean Energy

Housing is built using loval resources and are built to let natural cool air inside. Solar energy is also utilized for clean enerrgy.

Utilization of Mountain Breezes: Uses mountain breezes to naturally cool homes, to cool places without the need of expensive air conditioning that often increase the temperature more.

Use of Bamboo: Bamboo is used in some structures as a sustainable and low carbon replacement for concrete

Solar and Geothermal Power: Solar and Geothermal Energy combine to power electricity and make electricity grids cleaner.

COMMUNITY

Community Bayanihan & Fair Water Sharing

The humans present in the community are each responsible for only using their part of the resources. Making sure that no family is left behind in case of disasters

Split resources: Residents are given a calculation of their part of the resources, and are advised to only take their part and not exceed over it.

Community Support: Local vegetables are available and provide food for the people. Not much need for importing from other places.
