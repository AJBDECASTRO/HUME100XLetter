Core Ecosystem Criteria for 2055

How Los Baños balances public health, regenerative green engineering, natural restoration, and community equity as one living system.

Public Health & Clean Air

HEALTH

Without automotive smoke or factory exhaust, the air in Los Baños is clean and healthy. Mountain breezes cool our towers, and shaded high-rise breezeways encourage daily walking and neighborly connection.

  • Fresh Mountain Air: Natural air currents from Mount Makiling circulate freely through open-air towers.
  • Balcony Gardens: Community gardens on residential balconies produce fresh vegetables for families every day.
  • Cooling Breezeways: Public shaded mist corridors protect citizens during peak midday heat.

Smart Green Engineering

INFRASTRUCTURE

High-rise spires are constructed with self-healing bio-concrete containing natural limestone-producing bacteria. When moisture enters tiny cracks, bacteria activate to seal fractures naturally.

  • Self-Healing Concrete: High-rise towers heal their own structural stress cracks without toxic chemicals.
  • Geothermal Baselines: Clean geothermal heat from Mount Makiling supplies continuous power even during storms.
  • Carbon-Capturing Walls: Building facades absorb ambient CO2 from the surrounding atmosphere.

Restored Natural Commons

RESTORATION

Over 12 million native trees were replanted to reconnect Mount Makiling down to Laguna de Bay. A 300-meter ring of wetland reeds filters all urban runoff before it reaches the lake.

  • Natural Wetland Reeds: Bio-filtering reeds cleanse municipal water naturally, reviving fish populations.
  • Wildlife Skybridges: Elevated green bridges allow deer, birds, and forest fauna to migrate safely across town.
  • Protected Watershed: Zero-deforestation zones protect our fresh water supply for future generations.

Community Bayanihan & Equity

BAYANIHAN

Fair resource sharing ensures no family goes without clean water or power. Neighbors pool unused daily allowances to support elderly residents, and youth hold permanent seats in municipal decisions.

  • Resource Sharing Pools: Extra daily water and power credits are shared with households in need.
  • Youth Veto Power: Delegates aged 16–25 hold veto authority over long-term construction projects.
  • Weekly Assemblies: Community decisions are made collaboratively through open consensus.
